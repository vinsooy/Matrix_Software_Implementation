The matrix operation algorithms implemented are based on standard mathematical procedures:
- Matrix addition, multiplication, and transpose: Standard linear algebra operations
- Determinant calculation: Standard determinant formulas for 2x2 and 3x3 matrices
- Matrix inverse: Standard Gaussian elimination method

These algorithms are mathematical procedures in the public domain and not subject to copyright.
